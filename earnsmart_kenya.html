🛡️ Protect your money

How to identify online fraud

If an “opportunity” sounds too easy, slow down and verify it before sending money or personal information.

💸

Pay first, earn later

“Pay a registration, activation, training or withdrawal fee.” Legitimate jobs do not require you to pay someone to release wages.

📱

Random WhatsApp/Telegram job

Unexpected messages promising quick daily income are a major warning sign. Verify the company independently.

💰

Guaranteed huge income

Claims like “KES 10,000 every day with no experience” should make you stop and investigate.

🔐

Requests for sensitive data

Do not rush to send passwords, PINs, OTPs, card details or identity documents to an unverified recruiter.

🪙

Crypto deposit to unlock work

Task scams may show fake earnings and then demand your own money before you can withdraw. Walk away.

🔎

Verify before you trust

Search the company name with words such as “scam”, “review” or “complaint”, and visit the company's official site directly.

💼 Real online work

What can a beginner actually do?

You do not need to be an expert. You need one useful service, basic digital skills, consistency and a way to show your work.

⌨️

Data entry

Organise information in spreadsheets, copy information accurately and clean simple records.

🎨

Simple designs

Create flyers, social posts, menus and thumbnails with beginner-friendly design tools.

✍️

Writing & editing

Offer proofreading, captions, product descriptions, CV formatting and basic articles.

🎬

Short video editing

Trim clips, add captions, resize videos and create simple social-media edits.

📊

Spreadsheet help

Learn sorting, formatting, simple formulas and clean presentation in Excel or Google Sheets.

📱

Social media assistance

Schedule posts, write captions, organise content and help small businesses stay consistent.
